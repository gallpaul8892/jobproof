# Spec T-003i: Works with poor or no signal (offline queue)

Ticket: [tickets/T-003i-offline-and-poor-signal.md](../tickets/T-003i-offline-and-poor-signal.md).
Package E (may start once T-003g is merged, in parallel with T-003h/k). Design sections 3.3 to
3.5, 9. Kind: **mobile only** (+ test harness against the real backend). NFR-4 to NFR-8.

## Questions and assumptions

- A1. Pre-load: today's and earlier unfinished jobs only (ticket default).
- A2. No offline sign-in; an expired session offline keeps the app usable (T-003n).
- A3. Scenario 12 (stale edits) cannot produce a true conflict in this design: managers cannot
  see or edit drafts, operatives cannot edit submitted reports, and office checklist changes are
  merged by item id (T-003f). The only remaining case is two devices (last write wins per field,
  T3-5). "The office changed this report. Review" is therefore not built; this is recorded as a
  deliberate simplification.

## Summary

Harden the sync engine for a day without signal: offline banner, honest statuses, submit while
offline, background task, battery saver, restart safety, housekeeping; and the headless
integration suite that proves it without a phone.

## Mobile

- Connectivity from `expo-network` (`isInternetReachable`, type); banner within 10 s: "Offline.
  Your work is saved on this phone and will send when you're back in signal."; hidden on return.
- Offline submit: "Report saved. It will be submitted when you have signal."; job status
  "Submitted, waiting to send" (local `submit_queued`) distinct from "Submitted" (server
  confirmed). Never "Submitted" before the server says so.
- Order on reconnect: design 3.4 (photos first, then submit, stuck photos do not block).
- Background task `jobproof-sync` (`expo-background-task`, minimum interval 15 min): headless
  engine pass with a 25 s budget; registered after sign-in, unregistered at sign-out with nothing
  pending. "Open the app to finish sending" after 30 minutes without progress while backgrounded.
- Battery saver (`expo-battery` low power): automatic runs paused, line "Paused by battery saver.
  Tap Sync now."
- At most one status line; no pop-up per failure.
- Restart safety: all state in SQLite; in-flight uploads are restarted from `registered` (the
  server answers 200 if the file had arrived).
- Housekeeping (daily, at start): for submitted jobs older than 7 days with every photo `sent`,
  fetch `getOperativeJob`; when `reviewed_at` is more than 7 days ago, delete local files and rows
  for that job and drop its cache entries. Unsent items are never removed.
- Storage under 100 MB offline: capture refused (T-003e), queue kept.
- Clock warning (T-003k) works offline from the last measured offset.
