## Mobile

- Camera: first "Take photo" → explainer "This app needs your camera to photograph your work for
  the office" → Continue → system prompt (`Camera.requestCameraPermissionsAsync`). Denied once →
  "Without the camera you can only add photos from your gallery." with "Choose from gallery" and
  "Try again". Blocked (`canAskAgain` false) → "Camera is turned off for this app" with "Open
  settings" (`Linking.openSettings()`); on `AppState` active, re-check and open the camera
  without restart.
- Location (after the first camera grant): "Add location to your photos? It shows the office
  where the photo was taken. You can change this any time." → "Allow while using the app"
  (`requestForegroundPermissionsAsync`) / "Not now" (snooze 7 days). Never background; the
  `expo-location` plugin with `isAndroidBackgroundLocationEnabled: false`.
- Gallery uses the system photo picker; no media permissions (config test).
- Settings: "Camera: allowed / not allowed", "Location: allowed / not allowed" with "Open phone
  settings"; "No screen lock" notice from `expo-local-authentication`
  `getEnrolledLevelAsync()` (`NONE`) → "Your phone has no screen lock. Customer photos on it are
  less protected."; App version; Contact the office; Privacy.
- Privacy screen: text from `src/privacy/notice.ts` (what is collected, who sees it, not in the
  gallery or shared, retention per T3-1, contact). The same file is exported to
  `mobile/store/privacy-policy.md` for the Play listing (T-003p) by a script, and a test checks
  they match.
- Leaks: `expo-screen-capture` prevents capture and hides the recent-apps preview in all
  profiles except `screenshots`; a `log()` wrapper is a no-op in release and accepts only
  string codes and numbers (lint rule bans `console.*` in `mobile/src` and `mobile/app`);
  `allowBackup: false`.
